# Track geometry database — ODbL 1.0

The coordinates in `track-geometry.js` (the `REAL_TRACK_GEOMETRY` database) are
© OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ .

This file supplies the complete derived database used by the game, including
geographic centerlines for Circuit de la Sarthe, Fuji and Sebring and the
individual pit-lane ways for all 18 tracks. Way IDs are included with each track.

Extracted from the cached OpenStreetMap geometry in tobi/track-atlas, commit
8c12ac414ea74ffe4a3ef74b294a2eafab7ee60d:
https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/tree/8c12ac414ea74ffe4a3ef74b294a2eafab7ee60d/tracks

The database is available for reuse under ODbL, including the geographic source
coordinates before their projection into game coordinates. Game code and the
separate MIT-licensed F1 centerlines retain their respective existing licenses.
The geometry is community mapped, not a certified survey or endorsement by a
circuit, FIA, F1, WEC or ACO.
